# Title Craft

Guidelines for building strong titles: formats, structures, hooks, tone, length, and safe framing.

---

## Title Formats

### Numbered Format (Default)

Numbered titles are the preferred default when the topic naturally supports a list.

Recommended structures:

```
5 Alasan ...
5 Sebab ...
7 Kriteria ...
5 Kelebihan ...
7 Hal yang Perlu Diketahui ...
5 Cara ...
7 Tips ...
5 Kesalahan ...
7 Dampak ...
5 Biaya ...
7 Ide ...
5 Pilihan ...
```

### Non-Numbered Format

Use when the topic cannot be naturally expressed as a list:

```
Panduan [Topic] untuk [Audience]
Mengapa [Condition / Question]?
Bagaimana [Process] Bekerja?
Apa yang Terjadi Jika [Condition]?
[Topic]: Apa yang Perlu Diketahui Sebelum [Action]?
```

Do not force numbered format when it makes the title feel unnatural.

---

## Numbering Rules

Recommended numbers:

- **5** — most common, substantive enough
- **6** — use only if the topic genuinely yields 6 distinct points
- **7** — editorially strong
- **8** — for topics requiring broader coverage
- **10** — for comprehensive guides

Rules:

1. The number must represent a realistic count of genuinely distinct article sections or points.
2. Do not choose a large number simply to make the title appear more thorough.
3. Avoid unusual numbers (e.g. 11, 13) without a clear reason.

## Editorial Hooks

A hook is a framing element that makes the title more compelling without becoming misleading.

### Action Hooks

Active verbs that encourage action:

```
Tembus     → 5 Cara Tembus Target Tabungan Bulanan
Raih       → 7 Tips Raih Keuntungan Investasi Lebih Cepat
Pangkas    → 5 Cara Pangkas Pengeluaran Rumah Tangga
Maksimalkan → 7 Tips Maksimalkan Manfaat Kartu Kredit
Hindari    → 5 Hal yang Perlu Dihindari Saat Mengajukan KPR
```

### Practical Hooks

Framing that emphasizes ease or efficiency:

```
Anti Boncos   → 5 Cara Anti Boncos Mengatur Pengeluaran Bulanan
Anti Ribet    → 7 Tips Anti Ribet Memilih Motor untuk Harian
Hemat         → 7 Destinasi Wisata Bandung untuk Liburan Hemat
Mudah         → 5 Cara Mudah Daftar Paspor Online Tanpa Antre
```

### Curiosity Hooks

Framing that triggers interest:

```
Ternyata           → 5 Hal yang Ternyata Berpengaruh pada Cicilan KPR
Jarang Disadari    → 7 Kesalahan Keuangan yang Jarang Disadari
Yang Perlu Diketahui → 5 Hal yang Perlu Diketahui Sebelum Beli Mobil Bekas
Jangan Sampai Terlewat → 5 Biaya Tersembunyi KPR yang Jangan Sampai Terlewat
```

Use these as framing tools — not as permission to make claims the article cannot support.

### Lifestyle / Emotional Hooks

Framing that appeals to personal experience or lifestyle:

```
Short Escape   → 5 Destinasi Short Escape dari Jakarta yang Mudah Dijangkau
Weekend Escape → 7 Ide Weekend Escape ke Bandung untuk Keluarga
Bikin Kangen   → 5 Kuliner Lama Palembang yang Bikin Kangen
```

Use only when the topic is genuinely related to lifestyle or travel.

## Tone by Brief Context

Match the title tone to the context identified in the brief or mandatory column.

| Context | Tone | Example Title |
|---|---|---|
| **Soft selling / guest post** | Editorial, natural, not openly promotional | 5 Cara Belanja Hemat agar Tetap Cuan di Tengah Bulan |
| **Bisnis & Keuangan** | Serious, informative, analytical | 5 Faktor yang Memengaruhi Nilai Aset Kendaraan dari Waktu ke Waktu |
| **Health / Lifestyle** | Neutral, informative, empathetic | 7 Dampak Kurang Tidur yang Sering Diabaikan |
| **Travel** | Light, inspirational, personal | 7 Destinasi Wisata Sekitar Palembang yang Wajib Dikunjungi |
| **Automotive** | Lightly technical, practical | 5 Hal yang Perlu Dicek Sebelum Membeli Mobil Bekas |
| **Property / KPR** | Informative, cautious, financial | 5 Biaya Tersembunyi yang Perlu Diperhitungkan Saat Ambil KPR |
| **E-commerce / Shopping** | Light, practical, action-oriented | 5 Tips Belanja Online agar Tidak Kalap di Akhir Bulan |

## Title Length Guidelines

| Size | Word Count | Character Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Ideal** | 7–12 words | 45–70 characters |
| **Still acceptable** | 6–14 words | 40–80 characters |
| **Too short** | < 5 words | < 35 characters |
| **Too long** | > 15 words | > 90 characters |

For web and SEO distribution, target under 65 characters where possible.

Longer titles are acceptable when the topic genuinely requires more specification (e.g. a long product name that must appear in full).

## Safe Framing vs Problematic Framing

### Hook — Safe

A hook is editorial framing. It does not make a factual claim.

```
✓ 5 Cara Anti Boncos Mengatur Pengeluaran Bulanan
✓ 7 Tips Belanja Hemat agar Tetap Cuan di Tengah Bulan
✓ 5 Hal yang Perlu Diketahui Sebelum Membeli Honda Beat
```

### Claim — Potentially Problematic

A claim is a factual assertion that requires evidence.

```
✗ 5 Cara yang Dijamin Membuat Anda Anti Boncos
✗ Honda Beat Terbukti Paling Irit di Kelasnya
✗ 7 Rahasia Tersembunyi yang Pasti Mengubah Hidup Anda
```

### The Core Rule

> **Strong editorial framing is allowed. Unsupported factual certainty is not.**

---

## Claims to Avoid

Unless the supplied brief or verified data explicitly supports them:

```
Pasti Berhasil
Dijamin Hemat
Dijamin Kaya
Terbaik di Indonesia
Nomor 1
100% Aman
100% Anti Boncos
Pasti Cocok untuk Semua Orang
Dijamin Bikin Sukses
Terbukti Ampuh
Paling Murah
Paling Lengkap
```

## Soft Selling — Default Behavior

**Soft selling is the default approach for all titles**, regardless of the brief value — including briefs that indicate advertorial, paid content, or hard selling.

Brand names, platform names, and product keywords do **not** appear in the title by default.

They are introduced organically within the article body — not in the title.

### The Only Exception — Explicit Instruction

A brand or keyword enters the title **only** when brief or mandatory explicitly instructs it.

| Situation | Brand in title? |
|---|---|
| Brief: `"Softsell"` | No |
| Brief: `"Advetorial"` / `"paid content"` / `"hard selling"` | No |
| Mandatory: brand name only (e.g. `"GoPay Games"`) | No |
| Anchor text 1 is a brand name (e.g. `GoPay Games`) | No |
| Brief/mandatory: `"sebutkan [brand] di judul"` | Yes |
| Brief/mandatory: `"judul harus mengandung nama brand"` | Yes |
| Brief/mandatory: `"keyword anchor wajib muncul di judul"` | Yes |

### Prioritize Contextual Titles for Soft Sell

When the soft-sell default applies, **prefer Contextual titles** over Direct ones.

The anchor subject (product, brand, service, or platform) is not addressed directly in the title. Instead, the title enters through the user's underlying need, experience, problem, or goal. The anchor is introduced organically in the article body.

This principle applies across all content categories:

| Anchor | Direct (avoid) | Contextual (preferred) |
|---|---|---|
| `top up ml` | 5 Cara Top Up ML yang Mudah | Push Rank MLBB Jadi Susah? Ini Equipment yang Sering Diabaikan |
| `GoPay Games` | 5 Fitur GoPay Games yang Perlu Diketahui | Kantong Game Lo Jebol? Ini Cara Main Game Tanpa Harus Keluar Banyak |
| `tiket pesawat Jakarta-Jogja` | 5 Tips Beli Tiket Jakarta-Jogja Murah | Liburan ke Jogja Tanpa Bikin Kantong Jebol, Ini Rahasianya |
| `Bis Harapan Jaya` | 5 Keunggulan Bis Harapan Jaya | Perjalanan Malam Antar Kota Lebih Nyaman? Ini yang Perlu Disiapkan |
| `Hyundai Palisade` | 5 Kelebihan Hyundai Palisade | SUV Keluarga dengan Kabin Lega, Ini yang Bikin Road Trip Jadi Berbeda |

A Direct title is acceptable when no strong Contextual angle is available, or when the brief suggests an informational approach.

### Soft Sell Examples

**Anchor 1: top up ml / Brief: Softsell**
```
✓  Push Rank MLBB Jadi Susah? Ini 5 Item yang Sering Diabaikan
   (contextual — gameplay experience, top up introduced in article body)

✓  5 Cara Top Up Mobile Legends yang Lebih Efisien
   (direct — acceptable when no strong contextual angle found)
```

**Anchor 1: GoPay Games / Anchor 2: top up ml / Brief: Softsell**
```
✓  Skin Favorite Abis Restock? Jangan Sampai Kehabisan, Intip Strategi Ini
   (contextual — anchor 1 is brand but no explicit instruction, default applies)

❌ 5 Cara Top Up Mobile Legends Lebih Hemat via GoPay Games
   (brand in title — violates default, no explicit instruction present)
```

**Anchor 1: GoPay Games / Brief: Softsell + "sertakan GoPay Games di judul"**
```
✓  5 Cara Top Up Game Favorit Lebih Hemat dengan GoPay Games
   (explicit instruction — brand allowed in title)
```

The title should create a natural editorial context in which the promoted brand or product can be introduced organically within the article body.

## Handling Product or Brand Names in Titles

Anchor text containing a product or brand name can appear in the title directly or indirectly.

**Direct (name included):**
```
Anchor: STARGAZER CARTENZ
Title:  5 Kelebihan STARGAZER CARTENZ yang Relevan untuk Keluarga Urban
```

**Indirect / Contextual (name omitted):**
```
Anchor: STARGAZER CARTENZ
Title:  5 Hal yang Perlu Dicermati Saat Memilih MPV Keluarga di Kelas Menengah
```

Choose whichever approach produces the most natural title that fits the brief.

Do not force a product name into the title if it makes the title sound like a direct advertisement.

---

## Handling Anchor Text 2

When `anchor text 2` is available:

- Read it as supplementary information or a variant of anchor text 1
- Identify whether the two anchors are complementary or carry different angles
- Do not insert both literally into the title
- Use both to sharpen understanding of the topic and intent

Example:

```
Anchor 1: STARGAZER CARTENZ
Anchor 2: Harga baru Stargazer Cartenz
```

Insight: the audience is interested in this product from a purchase-decision or financial angle → the title may approach pricing, value, or feature-to-price ratio.
