# Anti-Patterns

Bad title patterns to avoid — with explanations and corrected examples.

---

## 1. Keyword Mirroring

### Definition

Copying the anchor text into the title without adding a meaningful editorial angle.

### Bad Examples

```
Anchor: KPR rumah
Title:  Informasi tentang KPR Rumah
```

```
Anchor: harga Honda Beat
Title:  Harga Honda Beat Terbaru dan Informasinya
```

```
Anchor: cara daftar paspor online
Title:  Cara Daftar Paspor Online
```

### Why It Fails

- The title adds no editorial value
- It gives the reader no compelling reason to open the article
- It sounds like metadata, not an article title

### Corrections

```
❌ Informasi tentang KPR Rumah
✓  5 Hal yang Perlu Disiapkan Sebelum Mengajukan KPR untuk Pertama Kali

❌ Harga Honda Beat Terbaru dan Informasinya
✓  5 Hal yang Perlu Diketahui Sebelum Membeli Honda Beat

❌ Cara Daftar Paspor Online
✓  Panduan Lengkap Daftar Paspor Online Tanpa Harus Antre di Kantor
```

## 2. Forced Contextual

### Definition

Creating a title that claims to be Contextual but whose connection to the anchor is too weak or cannot be explained logically.

### Bad Examples

```
Anchor: tiket pesawat Jakarta-Palembang
Title:  5 Tips Merawat Tanaman Hias di Rumah
```

```
Anchor: investasi saham pemula
Title:  7 Resep Masakan Praktis untuk Hari Sibuk
```

### Why It Fails

- There is no real semantic connection
- The article cannot organically support the title
- Readers arriving through the anchor link will find irrelevant content

### The Test

A valid Contextual relationship must be explainable through a logical chain:

```
Anchor → [logical step] → [logical step] → Title Topic
```

If this chain cannot be constructed naturally, do not use a Contextual relationship.

## 3. Keyword Stuffing

### Definition

Packing the title with keyword variations, synonyms, or unnecessary qualifiers.

### Bad Examples

```
5 Tips Memilih Tiket Pesawat Murah Jakarta Palembang untuk Perjalanan 
Pesawat Jakarta Palembang Terbaik
```

```
7 Cara Investasi Saham Pemula Belajar Investasi Saham untuk Pemula 
Baru Mulai Investasi
```

### Why It Fails

- The title reads as unnatural
- There is no single dominant editorial angle
- Readers cannot tell what the article is actually about

### Corrections

```
❌ 5 Tips Memilih Tiket Pesawat Murah Jakarta Palembang untuk Perjalanan Pesawat Jakarta Palembang
✓  5 Tips Mendapatkan Tiket Pesawat ke Palembang dengan Harga Terjangkau

❌ 7 Cara Investasi Saham Pemula Belajar Investasi Saham untuk Pemula Baru Mulai Investasi
✓  7 Langkah Memulai Investasi Saham bagi Pemula yang Baru Belajar
```

## 4. Unsupported Sensationalism

### Definition

Using exaggerated, absolute, or overblown framing that promises something the article cannot deliver.

### Bad Examples

```
7 Rahasia Tersembunyi yang Pasti Mengubah Hidup Anda Selamanya
5 Trik Ampuh yang Dijamin Membuat Anda Kaya dalam 30 Hari
10 Fakta Mengejutkan yang Tidak Pernah Diberitahu Siapapun
```

### Why It Fails

- The article cannot fulfill the outsized promise
- Readers feel deceived after reading
- It damages editorial credibility

### The Boundary

```
Strong framing that is still safe:
✓ 5 Kesalahan Investasi yang Bisa Menguras Tabungan Tanpa Disadari

Exaggerated framing:
✗ 5 Kesalahan Fatal yang Pasti Menghancurkan Seluruh Keuangan Anda
```

The difference: "bisa" (can) vs "pasti" (will certainly), "menguras" (drain) vs "menghancurkan seluruh" (destroy everything).

## 5. Unsupported Claims

### Definition

Inserting specific factual claims into the title without available supporting data.

### Claims to Avoid

```
Terbaik di Indonesia
Nomor 1
Paling Murah
Paling Lengkap
100% Aman
100% Anti Boncos
Paling Irit di Kelasnya
Terbukti Ampuh
Sudah Terbukti
Dijamin Berhasil
Pasti Cocok untuk Semua Orang
```

### Bad Examples

```
Anchor: Adidas Adizero Evo SL
Title:  Adidas Adizero Evo SL: Sepatu Lari Terbaik di Indonesia Saat Ini
```

```
Anchor: Honda Beat
Title:  Honda Beat, Motor Paling Irit yang Sudah Terbukti di Kelasnya
```

### Corrections

```
❌ Adidas Adizero Evo SL: Sepatu Lari Terbaik di Indonesia Saat Ini
✓  5 Kelebihan Adidas Adizero Evo SL yang Relevan untuk Runner Harian

❌ Honda Beat, Motor Paling Irit yang Sudah Terbukti di Kelasnya
✓  5 Alasan Honda Beat Masih Menjadi Pilihan Populer untuk Motor Harian
```

## 6. Title Too Long

### Definition

A title that contains too many elements: too many hooks, qualifiers, keywords, or benefits at once.

### Bad Examples

```
7 Tips Hemat Anti Boncos Belanja Online di Blibli agar Tetap Cuan dan 
Tidak Boros di Tengah Bulan Meski Gaji Sudah Menipis
```

```
5 Cara Memilih Sepatu Running Wanita yang Nyaman, Hemat, Berkualitas, 
Cocok untuk Olahraga Harian, dan Tidak Membebani Budget
```

### Why It Fails

- No single dominant editorial angle
- The title reads as a combination of two or three separate titles
- Readers are confused about what the article focuses on

### Corrections

```
❌ 7 Tips Hemat Anti Boncos Belanja Online di Blibli agar Tetap Cuan dan Tidak Boros di Tengah Bulan
✓  5 Cara Belanja Hemat agar Tetap Cuan di Tengah Bulan

❌ 5 Cara Memilih Sepatu Running Wanita yang Nyaman, Hemat, Berkualitas, Cocok untuk Olahraga Harian
✓  7 Tips Memilih Sepatu Running Wanita untuk Olahraga Harian
```

## 7. Advertising Copy as Title

### Definition

A title that reads like a product tagline or ad copy rather than an editorial article.

### Bad Examples

```
Blibli: Solusi Belanja Terbaik untuk Kebutuhan Anda Setiap Hari
Hyundai Stargazer Cartenz: Mobil Impian Keluarga Indonesia
KPR BCA: Wujudkan Rumah Impian Anda Sekarang
```

### Why It Fails

- Readers immediately recognize it as promotional content
- It loses editorial credibility
- It undermines the natural context a soft-selling article needs

### Corrections

```
❌ Blibli: Solusi Belanja Terbaik untuk Kebutuhan Anda Setiap Hari
✓  5 Cara Belanja Hemat agar Tetap Cuan di Tengah Bulan

❌ Hyundai Stargazer Cartenz: Mobil Impian Keluarga Indonesia
✓  5 Pertimbangan Finansial Sebelum Membeli MPV Keluarga di Kelas Menengah

❌ KPR BCA: Wujudkan Rumah Impian Anda Sekarang
✓  5 Hal yang Perlu Disiapkan Sebelum Mengajukan KPR untuk Pertama Kali
```

## 8. Unrealistic Numbers

### Definition

Using a large number in the title that cannot be fulfilled by genuinely distinct points.

### Bad Examples

```
17 Alasan Yamaha Gear 125 Adalah Motor Terbaik
15 Kelebihan Honda Beat yang Wajib Anda Ketahui
```

### Why It Fails

- 15–17 genuinely distinct points for one product are nearly impossible to produce
- The article is forced to repeat points or pad with trivial ones
- Readers notice the padding and lose trust

### Rule

Choose a number that is realistic for the actual distinct points available.

```
❌ 17 Alasan Yamaha Gear 125 Adalah Motor Terbaik
✓  5 Kelebihan Yamaha Gear 125 yang Relevan untuk Penggunaan Harian
```

## 9. Using Article Instructions as Title Context

### Definition

Treating article structure or content instructions from `mandatory` or `brief` as if they were title-generation context.

### Example of the Error

```
Mandatory: Bahas mobil Hyundai dalam H2 terpisah. Maksimal 3 paragraf.

Wrong title output:
"Hyundai Stargazer Cartenz dalam 3 Paragraf: Analisis Finansial MPV Baru"
```

### Why It Fails

- H2 structure and paragraph count are instructions for the writer, not for the title
- A title representing article structure sounds unnatural and non-editorial

### Correct Approach

```
Extract from mandatory/brief for title:
✓ Category: Bisnis & Keuangan
✓ Angle: financial calculation, asset depreciation

Ignore from mandatory/brief for title:
✗ H2 terpisah
✗ Maksimal 3 paragraf
```

## Anti-Pattern Summary

| Anti-Pattern | Warning Sign | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword Mirroring | Title only repeats the anchor | Add a genuine editorial angle |
| Forced Contextual | Connection cannot be logically explained | Return to Direct or find a stronger relationship |
| Keyword Stuffing | Multiple keyword variants in one title | Focus on one dominant angle |
| Unsupported Sensationalism | "Pasti", "Dijamin", "Mengubah Hidup" | Replace with more measured framing |
| Unsupported Claims | "Terbaik", "Nomor 1", "Paling Irit" | Remove the claim; focus on features or experience |
| Title Too Long | More than 80 characters | Trim to one dominant angle |
| Advertising Copy | Reads like a product tagline | Reframe as editorial content |
| Unrealistic Numbers | 15, 17, 20 points for one product | Reduce to 5–8 |
| Misreading Mandatory | Article instructions appear in the title | Separate article instructions from title context |
